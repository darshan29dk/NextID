# rAnalyzer — Advertisement Video Script
Audience: mentors / internal demo · Target length: ~2:45–3:00 · Tone: professional/corporate

---

**Scene 1 — The Problem** (title card)
> Every enterprise runs on access — thousands of accounts and entitlements, spread across dozens of applications. Over time, that access sprawls. Nobody can say with confidence who has what, or why. Provisioning takes weeks, audits take longer, and every unmanaged permission is a risk waiting to be found.

**Scene 2 — Introducing rAnalyzer** (title card)
> This is rAnalyzer — a role mining and governance platform that turns that sprawl into structure. It discovers the roles hiding inside your identity data, lets your team refine and own them, and keeps every one of them compliant, end to end.

**Scene 3 — Role Discovery: Mining** (real screenshot: Candidate Roles, filtered)
> It starts with Role Discovery. rAnalyzer scans real account and entitlement data, groups people by job function, and clusters them by the access they actually share. Each cluster becomes a candidate role, scored with a confidence level and a coverage percentage — so you know exactly how well it represents the people inside it.

**Scene 4 — Role Discovery: Analytical View** (real screenshot: Grid/dots matrix)
> Filter by department or application, and the Analytical View lays every matching role side by side — entitlements on one axis, members on the other — so you can see precisely who holds what, and why a role was formed the way it was. Nothing here is a black box. Every recommendation traces back to real data.

**Scene 5 — Role Engineering** (graphic)
> From there, Role Engineering lets your team shape a candidate role — reviewing its core entitlements, adjusting the confidence threshold, comparing variants side by side, and assigning an owner who's accountable for it going forward.

**Scene 6 — Governance & SoD** (graphic)
> Every role is checked against your Segregation of Duties policies automatically. rAnalyzer scans for toxic access combinations, flags violations before they become incidents, and surfaces it all on a live risk dashboard — so governance is a constant, running check, not a once-a-quarter scramble.

**Scene 7 — Approval Workflows** (graphic)
> Nothing goes live without accountability. Roles move through a structured approval workflow — owner review, then business sign-off. And if an owner is ever removed while a request is still pending, rAnalyzer automatically expires it, instead of leaving it stuck in limbo.

**Scene 8 — Role Catalog & Close** (title card)
> Approved roles publish straight to the Role Catalog, ready for provisioning — one trusted source of truth for who should have access to what. From messy identity data to a governed, audit-ready role model — that's rAnalyzer.
